GovIntel AI Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 18, 2026

Overview

GovIntel AI is a government sales intelligence platform that helps businesses identify federal buyers, analyze government spending, research competitors, and discover contracting opportunities.

Information We Collect

  • Questions and prompts submitted by users
  • Company information voluntarily provided by users
  • Industry information provided by users
  • Website URLs and capability statements submitted for analysis
  • Technical usage information required to operate the service

How We Use Information

  • Generate market intelligence reports
  • Identify government buyers and opportunities
  • Improve service quality and performance
  • Measure platform usage

Data Sources

GovIntel AI may use publicly available government data sources including USAspending.gov and other public procurement databases.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Data Retention

Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to provide services and improve platform performance.

Security

Reasonable safeguards are used to protect information submitted through GovIntel AI.
