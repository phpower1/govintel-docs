GovIntel AI User Guide

Learn how to identify government buyers, discover opportunities, analyze competitors, and build a winning federal sales strategy.

What is GovIntel AI?

GovIntel AI is an AI-powered Federal Sales Intelligence platform designed to answer one critical question:

"Who should I sell to next?"

GovIntel combines federal spending intelligence, market analysis, competitor research, agency targeting, and capture strategy recommendations to help companies pursue government contracts more effectively.

Tips for Best Results

  • Be specific about your products and services.
  • Provide your company website whenever possible.
  • Upload your capability statement for deeper analysis.
  • Mention certifications such as 8(a), HUBZone, WOSB, or SDVOSB.
  • Ask follow-up questions to refine recommendations.

Suggested Workflow

  1. Analyze your company.
  2. Identify target agencies.
  3. Analyze spending trends.
  4. Identify competitors and incumbents.
  5. Find recompete opportunities.
  6. Create a capture strategy.
